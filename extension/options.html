Tabcat settings

Tabcat groups related tabs and names the groups, on this computer. The first time, it downloads two small models (about 80 MB).

Grouping

When this is off, tabs are only grouped when you press Tidy tabs.

When this is off, it waits for a similar tab.

LooserStricter

Stricter makes smaller groups.

Groups you've renamed keep their name.

Your categories (optional)

Groups you always want, like Work or Shopping. Say what belongs in each.

Finding moved tabs

Show finds the tab in the tab bar. Undo puts it back.

Tabcat can't show messages while its access to websites is off (about:addons → Tabcat → Permissions).

Press to show the last tab Tabcat moved. Change shortcut

AI service (optional)

Tabcat only asks it about tabs it isn't sure of, many at a time.

Any OpenAI-compatible service: OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Ollama or LM Studio on this computer. It's sent tab titles and website names, and sometimes a page's description.
